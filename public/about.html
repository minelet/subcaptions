About Litix

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Litix is a creator toolkit built to save you editing time.

What it does

  • Viral Clips: upload a long recording such as a stream or podcast and get ready-to-post 9:16 clips ranked by how well they are likely to hold viewers.
  • Subtitles: auto-generated captions with animated templates, custom fonts and styles, exported straight from your browser.

How it works

Your video is processed in your browser. Only the audio needed for the transcript is sent for speech-to-text, and your video files are not stored.

Free to start

New accounts can use their first 10 credits with no watermark. Credits can be bought, and the watermark can be removed for a while by watching an ad.
